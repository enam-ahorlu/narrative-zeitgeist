How picks are matched

For You, Hidden Gems and New releases look at a sample of works you rated 7/10 or higher across the relevant formats. More like this uses the entry you choose. Tags on those entries help identify specific qualities to look for.

Close match means the pick was linked to an entry you rated at least 8/10 and the recommender suggested two or more shared tags. Shared taste indicates some tag overlap. Explore gives you a connection to consider when there’s no suggested overlap.
